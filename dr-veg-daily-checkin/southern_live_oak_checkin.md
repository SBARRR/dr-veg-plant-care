Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering to keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy, since it’s recovering from root disturbance and the recent rain was light. Keep it in its current bright outdoor spot with good airflow to help prevent fungal issues, and watch those orange spots closely for any worsening.

Top priority/action for today: Deep water the tree thoroughly until water drains freely from the pot’s drainage holes, then let it drain completely.

Watering guidance: Check the top 2–3 inches of soil—water if it feels dry or just slightly moist but not cool or damp.

One useful check: Inspect the upper leaves for any increase in orange powdery spots or new browning to catch fungal progression early.

Optional care improvement: Gently remove any fallen leaves or debris from the soil surface to improve airflow and reduce fungal risk.

Optional practical idea: Consider moving the pot slightly to maximize morning sun and avoid harsh afternoon heat if you notice leaf scorch signs.
