Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering to keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy, especially with the recent heat and low chance of rain. Keep an eye on those orange spots and browning leaves, but avoid any pruning or fertilizer until the tree shows stronger recovery signs.

Top priority/action for today: Deeply water the soil until water drains freely from the pot, then let it drain fully without standing water.

Watering guidance: Check the top 2–3 inches of soil for dryness before watering again; avoid letting the soil dry out completely or stay soggy.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any new orange powdery spots or worsening browning and note if the new growth looks healthy or stressed.

Optional care improvement: Increase airflow around the tree by gently repositioning it if the area feels stagnant to help reduce fungal issues.

Optional practical idea: Set a simple reminder to check soil moisture every 3–4 days during this hot period to avoid over- or under-watering.
