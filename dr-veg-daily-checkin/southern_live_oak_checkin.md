Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering to keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy, especially since it’s still recovering from transplant shock. Keep it in its current bright outdoor spot with good airflow to help it stay stable and avoid stressing it further.

Top priority/action for today: Deep water the tree thoroughly until water drains freely from the bottom of the pot, then let it drain completely.

Watering guidance: Check the top 2–3 inches of soil before watering; water only if it feels dry at that depth.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any new orange spots or browning and note if any new growth looks limp or wilted.

Optional care improvement: Gently remove any fallen leaves or debris from the soil surface to reduce fungal risk.

Optional practical idea: Consider moving the pot slightly if afternoon sun seems too intense, to prevent leaf scorch during the hottest hours.
