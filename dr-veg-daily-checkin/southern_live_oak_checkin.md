Southern Live Oak

Today, keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy since the oak is still recovering from transplant shock and root damage. Avoid fertilizing or pruning for now and focus on monitoring the orange spots and browning for any changes.

Top priority/action for today: Check soil moisture by feeling the top 2–3 inches and pot weight; water deeply only if the soil feels dry at that depth.

Watering guidance: When watering, soak thoroughly until water drains from the bottom, then let the pot drain fully—don’t let it sit in standing water.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any increase in orange powdery spots or browning and note if new growth looks healthy or stressed.

Optional care idea: Consider gently increasing airflow around the tree by repositioning it slightly if possible, to help reduce fungal pressure.

Optional practical idea: Mark a simple calendar reminder to check soil moisture every 3 days to avoid over- or under-watering during recovery.
