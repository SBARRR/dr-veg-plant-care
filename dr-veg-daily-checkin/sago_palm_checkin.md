Today, let the soil dry out a bit before you water again since the last rain was nearly a month ago and the pot is shallow, which can dry faster in this heat. Keep an eye on the older yellowing fronds but don’t remove them yet—they’re still helping the plant. With the sun still strong, make sure the plant isn’t getting scorched by harsh afternoon rays.

Top priority/action for today: Check the top 1–2 inches of soil moisture and only water if it feels dry and the pot feels noticeably lighter.

Watering guidance: When watering, soak thoroughly until water drains out the bottom, then empty any water collected in the drip tray to avoid soggy roots.

One useful check: Inspect the undersides of fronds and the crown for any early signs of scale or pests since this can sneak up even without obvious symptoms.

Optional care improvement: If you want to reduce heat stress, consider moving the sago to a spot with filtered afternoon sun or morning sun only.

Optional practical idea: Gently brush off any dust or debris from the fronds to help the plant breathe better and look fresher.
