Venus Flytrap

Today, keep the tray water level steady with distilled or rainwater to maintain consistently wet media, especially since it's warm and sunny outside. Avoid letting the soil dry out or the tray go empty, as this plant thrives with wet roots in bright light.

Top priority/action for today: Check the tray reservoir and refill with distilled or rainwater if the water level is low or the tray is dry.

Watering guidance: Keep the soil moist but not flooded; the media should feel wet to damp at all times.

One useful check: Inspect traps for any fully black or dead ones and gently remove only those without disturbing green tissue.

Optional care improvement: If you notice any algae or green film on the media surface, lightly brush it away without disturbing the roots.

Optional practical idea: Place the pot where it gets morning sun and some afternoon shade to avoid overheating in the peak heat.
