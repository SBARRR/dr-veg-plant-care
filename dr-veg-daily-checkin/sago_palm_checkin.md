Sago Palm

Today, let the rain keep your sago palm hydrated—no need to water since it got a good soaking recently. Keep an eye on drainage to avoid soggy roots, especially with the heavy rain and high humidity.

Top priority/action for today: Check that the pot’s drainage holes are clear and water is not pooling in the drip tray under the pot.

Watering guidance: Only water when the top 1–2 inches of soil feel dry and the pot feels noticeably lighter; avoid watering just because the surface looks dry.

One useful check: Inspect the older yellowing fronds for any signs of softening or pest presence, but leave them unless they become mostly brown or pull away easily.

Optional care improvement: If the sun gets too intense this afternoon, consider moving the sago to a spot with a bit more filtered light to prevent scorch.
