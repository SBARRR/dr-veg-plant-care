Desert Rose

Today, give your Desert Rose a thorough watering since the soil has had plenty of time to dry out after the last rain and watering events. Keep it in its sunny spot but watch for any signs of too much moisture given the humid conditions lately.

Top priority/action for today: Water deeply until water drains from the bottom, then let the pot drain completely and avoid standing water.

Watering guidance: Only water when the top 2-3 inches of soil feel dry and the caudex is firm, not soft or mushy.

One useful check: Feel the caudex firmness to ensure it’s still solid and not showing signs of rot or softness.

Optional care improvement: If you want to boost growth, consider a half-strength liquid fertilizer next week, but only if the plant stays healthy and firm.

Optional practical idea: Keep an eye on leaf spots or pests since the previous leaf treatment; catch any issues early by inspecting undersides of leaves.
