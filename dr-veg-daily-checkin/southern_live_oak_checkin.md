Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering to keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy, especially with the warm and sunny weather. Keep it in its current bright spot but watch for any signs of heat stress on the leaves.

Top priority/action for today: Deep water the tree thoroughly until water drains from the bottom of the pot, then let it drain fully.

Watering guidance: Check the top 2–3 inches of soil for moisture before watering again; avoid letting the soil dry out completely or stay soggy.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any new orange spots or browning and note if leaf posture looks healthy or wilted.

Optional care improvement: Gently remove any fallen leaves or debris from the soil surface to improve airflow and reduce fungal risk.

Optional practical idea: Consider moving the pot slightly to maximize morning sun and avoid the hottest afternoon rays if you notice leaf scorch signs.
