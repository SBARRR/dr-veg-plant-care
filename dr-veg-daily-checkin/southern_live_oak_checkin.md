Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering to keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy, especially since it’s recovering from transplant shock and the weather is warm with moderate humidity. Keep it in its current sunny spot but watch for any worsening orange spots or browning on the leaves.

Top priority/action for today: Deep water the tree thoroughly until water drains from the bottom, then let the pot drain fully—avoid watering again until the top 2–3 inches of soil start to feel dry.

Watering guidance: Check soil moisture by feeling the top 2–3 inches and lifting the pot to gauge weight before watering again.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any increase in orange powdery spots or browning and note if new growth looks healthy or stressed.

Optional care improvement: Gently brush off any powdery residue on leaves with a soft cloth or spray with a gentle mist of water to reduce fungal spores without stressing the tree.

Optional practical idea: Set a simple reminder to check soil moisture every 3–4 days to avoid over- or under-watering during this recovery phase.
