Southern Live Oak

Today, keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy—your oak is still recovering from transplant shock and root damage, so steady moisture is key. Avoid fertilizing or pruning for now; focus on monitoring the orange spotting and browning closely.

Top priority/action for today: Check soil moisture by feeling 2–3 inches below the surface and water deeply only if it feels dry at that depth.

Watering guidance: Water slowly until you see water draining from the pot’s holes, then let the pot drain fully—don’t let it sit in standing water.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves near the top shoot for any increase in orange powdery spots or new browning and note any changes.

Optional care improvement: Gently increase airflow around the tree by ensuring it’s not crowded by other plants to reduce fungal pressure.

Optional practical idea: Set a reminder to check soil moisture every 2–3 days during this hot spell to avoid both drought stress and soggy soil.
