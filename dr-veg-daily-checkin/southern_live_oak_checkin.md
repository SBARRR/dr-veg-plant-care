Southern Live Oak

Today, keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy—your oak is still recovering from transplant shock and root damage, so steady moisture is key. Avoid fertilizing or pruning for now; just focus on gentle care and monitoring the orange spotting and browning.

Top priority/action for today: Check soil moisture by feeling the top 2–3 inches and pot weight, then water deeply if the soil feels dry at that depth.

Watering guidance: Water thoroughly until water drains freely from the bottom, then let the pot drain completely without sitting in water.

One useful check: Inspect the leaves for any increase in orange spotting or new browning and note any changes in leaf posture or wilting.

Optional care improvement: Gently increase airflow around the tree if possible to reduce humidity around leaves and discourage fungal issues.

Optional practical idea: Consider moving the pot to a spot with slightly more morning sun but avoid harsh afternoon heat to help recovery without scorch risk.
