Southern Live Oak

Today, give your Southern Live Oak a deep watering since the soil is likely drying out under the warm, mostly sunny conditions. Keep the pot draining well and avoid letting the soil get soggy. Keep an eye on those orange spots and any new browning to catch changes early.

Top priority/action for today: Deep water the tree thoroughly until water drains freely from the pot holes, then let it drain completely.

Watering guidance: Check the top 2–3 inches of soil for dryness before watering again; aim for steady moisture without sogginess.

One useful check: Inspect the upper leaves for any increase in orange powdery spots or browning and note if new leaves look healthy.

Optional care improvement: Consider gently increasing airflow around the tree if possible to reduce leaf fungus risk.

Optional practical idea: Mark your calendar for a light fertilizing session next spring once the tree shows strong new growth and recovery.
