Venus Flytrap

Today, keep the tray water level steady to maintain consistently wet media, especially with the warm weather and moderate humidity. Avoid letting the media dry out, and enjoy watching those traps deepen their red color with the good light.

Top priority/action for today: Check the tray reservoir and refill with distilled or rainwater if the water level is low or the media feels less than damp.

Watering guidance: Keep the soil media wet at all times by bottom watering; do not let the media dry out or become crunchy.

One useful check: Inspect traps for any fully black or dead ones and remove only those, leaving green tissue intact.

Optional care improvement: If you notice any algae on the media surface, gently remove it without disturbing the roots or media too much.

Optional practical idea: Place the plant where it gets morning sun and some afternoon shade to avoid overheating in the hottest part of the day.
