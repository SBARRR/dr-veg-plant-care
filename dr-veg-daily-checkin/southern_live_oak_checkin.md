Southern Live Oak

Today, keep the soil evenly moist but not soggy since the oak is still recovering from transplant shock and root damage. Avoid fertilizing or pruning for now; focus on steady hydration and monitoring leaf health.

Top priority/action for today: Check soil moisture by feeling the top 2–3 inches and water deeply only if it feels dry to the touch.

Watering guidance: Water thoroughly until water drains from the bottom, then let the pot drain fully; avoid letting the soil stay wet for days.

One useful check: Inspect new growth and leaves for any increase in orange spotting or browning and note if leaf posture changes.

Optional care improvement: Consider gently increasing airflow around the tree if possible to reduce leaf wetness and fungal risk.

Optional practical idea: Keep a simple reminder to check soil moisture every 3–4 days to avoid over- or under-watering during recovery.
