Desert Rose

Today, keep your Desert Rose dry and warm since it’s still in recovery from the big root prune and repot. Hold off on watering or fertilizing until the soil is fully dry and the caudex feels firm again.

Top priority/action for today: Do not water yet; wait until the soil is completely dry and the caudex is firm before watering.

Watering guidance: Check the pot weight and soil dryness by feeling the top 2-3 inches; only water deeply when the soil is dry and the caudex is slightly shriveled but still firm.

One useful check: Gently press the caudex and lower stem area to confirm firmness—avoid any softness or mushiness that could signal rot.

Optional care improvement: Keep the plant in bright, indirect light to avoid sun stress while it recovers.
